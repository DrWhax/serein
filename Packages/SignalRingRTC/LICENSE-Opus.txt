The Swift SILK and CELT implementations use the normative tables and algorithms
from Xiph.Org's opus-1.5.2 release. The source archive is

https://downloads.xiph.org/releases/opus/opus-1.5.2.tar.gz
SHA-256 65c1d2f78b9f2fb20082c38cbe47c951ad5839345876e46941612ee87f9a7ce1

Tables come from silk/tables_{NLSF_CB_NB_MB,NLSF_CB_WB,gain,LTP,pitch_lag,
other,pulses_per_block}.c, silk/pitch_est_tables.c, silk/table_LSF_cos.c and
silk/resampler_rom.{c,h}. Speech parameter and synthesis references are
silk/decode_{indices,pulses,parameters,core,pitch}.c, silk/shell_coder.c,
silk/code_signs.c, silk/NLSF_{unpack,decode,stabilize}.c, silk/NLSF2A.c,
silk/LPC_fit.c, silk/gain_quant.c, silk/log2lin.c, silk/dec_API.c and
silk/resampler*.c.
