go-vp9 — pure-Go VP9 video decoder.

This is an original Go implementation of the VP9 decoding process, written from
the public VP9 Bitstream & Decoding Process Specification (Google / WebM Project,
v0.6) and informed for cross-checking by the BSD-3-Clause reference decoder
libvpx (https://chromium.googlesource.com/webm/libvpx, © The WebM Project authors).

VP9 is an open, royalty-free codec (royalty-free patent grant by Google). No
proprietary source or disassembly was used.

Reference material:
  - VP9 Bitstream & Decoding Process Specification (public, WebM Project).
  - libvpx (BSD-3-Clause) — used only as a behavioural reference for bit-exact
    cross-checking of intermediate values, not copied.
